Research

Fair distillation for vision-language models

Ongoing

Undergraduate Research Assistant, under Prof. Deepak Mishra — IIT Jodhpur

The question I'm working on: if a teacher model is biased, can you still train a fair student from it? I'm using Qwen2.5-VL-7B as the teacher and Qwen2.5-VL-3B as the student, starting with offline sparse logit distillation to establish a naive-distillation baseline, before applying Direct Steering Optimization (DSO) to explicitly steer the student away from inherited bias.

Early stage.
